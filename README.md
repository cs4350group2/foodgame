# Let Me Cook
Ready to run a restaurant?
In Let Me Cook, you’re in charge of keeping a busy restaurant running. Take customer orders, gather ingredients, and prepare meals at your stations. Then serve each dish before customers run out of patience.

Cooking puts your timing to the test in a rhythm minigame, where your performance affects dish quality. Earn money, meet special customers, and see if you can keep the kitchen moving when the rush hits.

Download the game, step into the kitchen, and give Let Me Cook a try!
